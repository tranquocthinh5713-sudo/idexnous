A Day at the Beach

Last Sunday, Anna went to the beach with her family. The weather was sunny and warm. They played volleyball, collected seashells and swam in the sea. At noon, they had lunch under a big tree. Anna took many beautiful photos. She felt very happy because she had a wonderful day with her family.

Comprehension check

1. Where did Anna go last Sunday?

Grammar

Ôn tập ngữ pháp tiếng Anh.

Present Simple – Thì hiện tại đơn

Công thức:

Khẳng định: S + V(s/es) + O.

Phủ định: S + do/does + not + V.

Nghi vấn: Do/Does + S + V?

Ví dụ: She goes to school every day.

Past Simple – Thì quá khứ đơn

Khẳng định: S + V2/ed + O.

Phủ định: S + did not + V.

Nghi vấn: Did + S + V?

Ví dụ: They visited the museum yesterday.
